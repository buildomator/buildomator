<objective>
Wire the session-start firing point. A SessionStart detector hook sits beside
gsd-staleness-reminder.js, runs the Plan 01 mechanical sweep over decisions whose hash is not
yet in the ledger, and when any has a contradiction candidate writes a pending-conflict record
under `.planning/` and emits a one-directional systemMessage pointing the agent at it. The
record is the carrier; the agent drains it, asks the user to confirm, and applies the rewrite
only on a yes. A small on-demand skill hosts the same drain-and-apply so it is runnable by
hand.

Purpose: delivers RECON-03 (session-start surfacing, always ask, never a silent rewrite) and
reinforces RECON-05 (a session with no new decisions does no model work because the sweep reads
the ledger first).

Output: hooks/gsd-reconcile-sweep.js, its registration in hooks.json, and skills/reconcile.
</objective>
